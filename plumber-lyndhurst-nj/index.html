Storm season and low ground in Lyndhurst

Lyndhurst sits about ten feet above sea level, according to its published geography, and the eastern part of the township is Meadowlands wetland. When heavy rain arrives, groundwater rises and sewers load up. A sump pump that fails then is the difference between a wet floor and a flooded basement.

Who handles what in Lyndhurst

Lyndhurst's Department of Public Works says the township buys its water in bulk from the Passaic Valley Water Commission and owns and runs its own water utility, with about 45 miles of mains and 5,700 service connections. Its Sewer Department maintains sewer mains, catch basins, manholes and sewer laterals.

That matters when a line fails. Before paying for a dig, call Public Works at (201) 438-5478 to ask where responsibility ends for your address. For after-hours water or sewer problems, the township directs residents to the police non-emergency line, (201) 939-2900. We can still come out for the plumbing inside your property line, day or night.
