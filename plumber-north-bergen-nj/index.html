What the hills and the Meadowlands mean for your plumbing

North Bergen sits on the Hudson Palisades, which makes it one of the hilliest towns in the state, while the low west side drops into the Meadowlands. Water runs downhill, so a basement or ground-floor garage on the low side of a slope takes runoff first, and a street-level floor drain can back up in heavy rain even when the sewer line is clear.

That is why we ask where the water appears and when. A backup during a storm points at drainage. A backup on a dry day points at a clog, and the fix is different. For clogs, see drain cleaning. For water that keeps returning to a lower level, the sump pump page explains pumps and backups.
