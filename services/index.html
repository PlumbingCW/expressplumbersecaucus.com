How to choose the right service

Water that is running, rising or spreading is an emergency. Close the main shutoff first. A slow drain, a dripping faucet or a toilet that keeps running can wait for a same-day visit. A rotten egg smell near a gas appliance is different: leave the home and call the gas utility from outside.

If the problem could be several things, such as a wet ceiling or a falling water pressure, start with leak detection. It finds where water is escaping before anyone opens a wall.

Still unsure? Call (551) 249-3045 and a dispatcher will sort it out with you in a minute or two. If the problem is a flooded basement, say so first, because the order of the first steps changes.
