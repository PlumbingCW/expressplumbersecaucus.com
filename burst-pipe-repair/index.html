Burst pipe emergency: your first ten minutes

What you do before we arrive limits damage.

  1. Shut the main

    Usually near the meter, the water heater or the front wall. Turn it fully.

  2. Drain the lines

    Open the lowest faucet and flush a toilet.

  3. Power and heater

    Turn off the heater. Shut off power to wet areas only if you can reach the panel dry.

  4. Contain

    Move valuables, mop, and use towels or a wet vac.

  5. Photograph

    Take photos before cleanup for an insurance claim.

What causes pipes to burst

Pipes fail for a handful of reasons, and the cause decides the repair.

Common causes and repairs

FreezingBurst in an exterior wall, garage or crawlspace in cold weatherCut out the split, replace the section, insulate

Copper, PEX and other repairs

Copper can be cut and fitted with new sections and soldered fittings. PEX can be repaired with crimp or expansion fittings. Galvanized steel is brittle, and a repair often reveals more weak spots nearby. If a house has old galvanized or failing supply lines, ask us about repiping after the emergency is over.

Frozen and condo pipes

In winter, pipes in exterior walls, unheated garages and crawlspaces freeze first. Thawing a frozen pipe safely means slow heat and an open faucet, and a visual check for splits as it thaws. In a condo, a burst pipe above or beside your unit may be the building's riser or a neighbor's line. Call the management office as well as us so the building's shutoff can be used.

Burst pipes in Secaucus homes

Many Secaucus residents live in stacked units, where one burst pipe reaches several floors. Know where your unit's shutoff is and where the riser shutoff for the building is, and keep the management emergency number near the phone. Insurance claims go more smoothly with photos and a record of when you shut the water off.

If the repair involves opening walls, permit rules vary, so ask the Secaucus construction department. The department is at the Town Hall Annex, 20 Centre Avenue.

Codes, permits and records for a pipe repair

Repairs that replace a section of supply pipe are covered by the National Standard Plumbing Code, 2021 edition, which New Jersey adopts as its plumbing subcode in N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15, and larger replacements may need a permit. Ask the Secaucus construction department at (201) 330-2027. Keep the plumber's written description of the failure, since an insurer or a condo association will often ask for it.
