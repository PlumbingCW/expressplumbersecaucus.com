What changes from town to town

Every town has its own permit office, its own utility and its own building mix. Secaucus has about 9,316 housing units, many in condos and townhouses. North Bergen is hilly and dense. Jersey City adds large apartment buildings and old rowhouses. Lyndhurst and Rutherford have older single-family homes with basements and aging sewer laterals.

We read those differences before we arrive. A call from a Jersey City high-rise is a different job from a call from a Rutherford basement, even when the symptom sounds the same. If you need emergency help now, call (551) 249-3045 and name your town.
