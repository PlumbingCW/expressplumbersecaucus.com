Commercial Plumber at Harmon Meadow, Secaucus, NJ

Harmon Meadow is Secaucus's hotel, restaurant, shopping and office district, off Route 3 near the New Jersey Turnpike. It is a commercial district, not a residential subdivision, and this page is for the businesses and property managers who work in it. The Harper at Harmon Meadow, a 469-unit apartment complex, sits in its northeastern corner.

Plumbing here is usually a coordination job as much as a repair. To talk through a problem at a restaurant, hotel, store or office suite, call (551) 249-3045.

What Harmon Meadow is

The Plaza at Harmon Meadow is a large hotel, restaurant and shopping complex that includes the Meadowlands Convention Center, a 14-screen movie theater and a Walmart and Sam's Club. Hartz Mountain Industries, the developer, reports about 30 restaurants, a convention center and a mix of hotels, retail and office space. Published counts of hotel rooms differ by source, so we do not give one. Mill Creek Mall is nearby, on Route 3 west of the Turnpike.

Why commercial plumbing differs

A commercial building has more fixtures, more occupants and often shared systems. A leak in one suite can affect another, and a single drain line may serve several tenants. That is general information about commercial buildings, not a claim about particular Harmon Meadow properties. In a multi-tenant building the landlord or property manager usually controls the main shutoff and the building's risers.

  • Ask the property manager who can close the main and the riser valves.
  • Ask whether work in a shared ceiling or wall needs the landlord's written approval.
  • Ask whether contractors must show proof of insurance to enter the building.
  • Ask which hours are allowed for noisy or disruptive work.

Apartments in the district

The Harper at Harmon Meadow has 469 apartments in three midrise buildings at 100 Park Plaza Drive, so the district is not only commercial. Residents of a managed apartment building should report leaks and drain problems to the building office first. For a unit-level problem, our Harmon Cove page explains condo and townhouse questions that apply to apartments too.

Permits, utilities and the town

Permit rules vary, so ask before work starts. The Secaucus construction department is at the Town Hall Annex, 20 Centre Avenue, (201) 330-2027, Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. Sewer rules come from the Secaucus Municipal Utilities Authority, and restaurants should ask it about grease rules.
