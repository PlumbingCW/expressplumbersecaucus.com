Emergency Hydro Jetting in Secaucus, NJ

Emergency hydro jetting in Secaucus is the fix for a sewer or drain line that keeps clogging because grease, scale or roots have narrowed the pipe, and a cable only drills a hole through the middle. A jet sends high-pressure water down the line and scours the wall. Our dispatch is open 24/7.

Sewer backup emergency: what to do first

Treat a backup as urgent when more than one fixture is affected, when a floor drain overflows, or when sewage smell fills a basement.

  • Stop running the washer, dishwasher and showers.
  • Do not flush, and do not pour chemical cleaner into standing water.
  • Move stored boxes, rugs and electronics away from floor drains.
  • Keep people and pets out of any room with sewage on the floor.
  • Take a photo for your insurer if water has reached finished space.

After-hours calls get the same questions as daytime ones. We ask which fixtures back up, whether it has happened before, and whether a cleanout is reachable.

What hydro jetting does that a cable cannot

A cable machine spins a cutting head through the blockage. It opens the pipe, but it leaves a ring of grease or scale on the wall, and that ring is what catches the next clog. A jet nozzle with rear-facing ports pulls itself down the line and blasts the wall clean as it returns.

  1. Look first

    We ask about pipe age and material, and often run a camera to check the wall before applying pressure.

  2. Set pressure

    Pressure and nozzle are matched to the pipe, not run at full power by default.

  3. Jet down and back

    The nozzle scours grease, scale and root hair on the way back out.

  4. Verify

    We run water and re-scope if needed so you can see the pipe wall is clear.

When jetting is the wrong tool

Jetting is not for every pipe. Cracked, offset or collapsed clay and old cast iron can be damaged by pressure, and a pipe that has already failed needs repair rather than cleaning. That is why a camera inspection sometimes comes first. If the camera shows a break, you will hear about sewer line repair options instead of a jet.

For a one-time stoppage in a healthy line, a cable is often enough and costs less. We will tell you which one your line needs.

Jetting in Secaucus buildings

Harmon Cove was developed in the 1970s as townhouses and high-rise residential buildings. Buildings from that era commonly mix cast iron and plastic drain pipe, and cast iron collects scale inside. If your building has stacked units sharing a line, a backup in one unit often traces to a shared stack, so tell us whether neighbors are affected.

Restaurants and other commercial kitchens in the Meadowlands corridor send grease into lines that sit below a floor slab, and a quarterly jet can cost less than repeated emergency clears. The commercial plumbing page covers after-hours work for businesses.

Code and sewer rules for jetting

Drain and sewer work inside a property follows the National Standard Plumbing Code, 2021 edition, which New Jersey adopts as its plumbing subcode in N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15. Past the property line, Secaucus sewer rules apply. Jetting pressure and nozzle choice depend on pipe material, so ask the technician what the camera showed first.
