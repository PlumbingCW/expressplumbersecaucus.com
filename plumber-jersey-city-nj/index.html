The Heights and Journal Square: where we are busiest

Jersey City Heights sits on the Palisades, above Hoboken to the east and the Meadowlands to the west. The neighborhood was Hudson City before it joined Jersey City in 1869, and its housing is mostly two- and three-family houses and low-rise apartment buildings. Journal Square, a mixed-use district created in 1923, has a major transit hub and a lot of new residential construction.

That mix means very different plumbing: older multi-family houses with their own pipes and drains, and newer towers with shared risers. We ask which one you live in before dispatch.

If you rent in Jersey City

Renters often cannot choose the plumber, but a clear description helps the landlord act fast. Take photos, note when the problem began and tell the landlord or manager in writing. Jersey City details that affect plumbing

Jersey City is the Hudson County seat and had about 292,000 residents in 2020. Its water came from the Boonton Reservoir on the Rockaway River starting in 1904. The city's municipal utilities authority has run a lead service line replacement program, so ask your landlord or the utility whether your service line has been checked.

Hoboken borders Jersey City to the north, and we also reach it from Secaucus. Permit rules vary, so ask Jersey City's construction office what your job needs.
